## Objective

Implement the headless Python Playwright + FFmpeg browser video recorder (`tools/demos/web.py`) capturing clean `1280x720` `.mp4` and palette-optimized `.gif` walkthroughs of (1) configuring Direct Vertex AI (`/endpoints/openapi`, `google/gemini-3.8-flash`) inside Oracle APEX 26.1 Workspace Utilities and (2) opening/running the Vertex-AI-powered Cymbal Coffee APEX 26.1 app (`APEX_AI.GENERATE`), wired into `manage.py demos` and `Makefile`.

## Context

- `adb-free:latest-26ai` serves APEX 26.1.5 over self-signed HTTPS at `https://localhost:8443/ords/apex`, requiring Playwright `browser.new_context(ignore_https_errors=True, viewport={"width": 1280, "height": 720}, record_video_dir=..., record_video_size={"width": 1280, "height": 720})` and explicit `context.close()` before reading `page.video.path()`.
- Injecting a lightweight `add_init_script` cursor indicator, click-ripple animation, and bottom-right step caption banner makes headless browser captures legible in documentation without post-production editing.
- Two-pass `ffmpeg` transcoding produces universally playable H.264 `yuv420p` `+faststart` `.mp4` files and crisp `palettegen=stats_mode=diff` / `paletteuse=dither=bayer:bayer_scale=5:diff_mode=rectangle` `.gif` previews.
