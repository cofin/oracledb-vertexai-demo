## Objective

Restore the deterministic grounded route handlers that existed on `main` (`PRODUCT_RAG`, `PRODUCT_AVAILABILITY`, `STORE_LOCATION`, `ORDER_STATUS`) in `src/app/domain/chat/services/_grounded.py` and the `SQLPhase` / `SearchMetrics` builders in `src/app/domain/chat/services/_telemetry.py`, upgraded to consume `HybridTurnContext` (pre-fetched RRF products/stores and `term_hits` off-menu brand detection).

## Context

- On `main`, `src/app/domain/chat/services/{adk,_adk_grounding,_adk_support}.py` handled deterministic grounding for `PRODUCT_RAG`, `PRODUCT_AVAILABILITY`, `STORE_LOCATION`, and `ORDER_STATUS`, including:
  - Constrained `PRODUCT_RAG` synthesis that refuses to invent off-menu items and explicitly flags when a requested brand/term (such as `"Folgers"`) has zero catalog hits (`term_hits[term] == 0`), offering the closest in-catalog Cymbal Coffee alternatives (`SLIDES.md` Appendix B).
  - `STORE_LOCATION` and `PRODUCT_AVAILABILITY` handlers using `StoreService`, `InventoryService`, and `MapsService` with request-scoped browser coordinates (`ChatLocationInput`) and no-key Google Maps URLs (`CP-06`).
  - `ORDER_STATUS` returning the explicit unsupported-capability message until order tables exist.
  - Structured `sql_phases` (`hybrid_rrf`, `lexical_terms`, `store_distance`, `inventory_lookup`, `response_cache`) and `SearchMetrics` (`similarity_scores`, `avg_similarity`, `products_Simple`, `embedding_time_ms`, `vector_search_time_ms`) rendered by `src/resources/telemetry.js` (`CP-02`, `CP-05`).
