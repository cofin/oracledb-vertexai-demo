## Objective

Create `src/app/db/sql/hybrid.sql` containing `hybrid-search-turn` (single-query RRF fusion over `chat_route`, `product`, and `coffee_store` combining `CONTAINS`/`SCORE` and `VECTOR_DISTANCE` `FETCH APPROX`) and `hybrid-term-hits` (`JSON_TABLE` per-term lexical hit counts on `product`), register `hybrid.sql` in `src/app/config.py`, and implement `build_oracle_text_query`, `extract_candidate_terms`, and `HybridRouteResolver` in `src/app/domain/chat/services/_router.py` backed by a 30+ prompt golden routing suite (`src/tests/fixtures/routing_golden.jsonl`).

## Context

- See promoted research [`research_oracle_hybrid_rrf_20261012`](../research/research_oracle_hybrid_rrf_20261012/research.md) for the verified Oracle 26ai RRF query and `build_oracle_text_query` escaping rules.
- Every chat turn embeds the user prompt once (`embedding_purpose="query"`, cached in `embedding_cache`) and runs `hybrid-search-turn` + `hybrid-term-hits`.
- `HybridRouteResolver` inspects the fused `route`, `product`, and `store` rows plus `term_hits` to return a `HybridTurnContext` containing:
  - `intent_detected`: `"PRODUCT_RAG" | "PRODUCT_AVAILABILITY" | "STORE_LOCATION" | "ORDER_STATUS" | "GENERAL_CONVERSATION"`
  - `is_deterministic`: `bool` (whether to dispatch directly to a grounded handler or delegate to `AgentRuntime`)
  - `route_confidence`: `float`
  - `products`: `list[HybridProductCandidate]` (with `rrf_score`, `lexical_score`, `vector_similarity`, `lexical_rank`, `vector_rank`)
  - `stores`: `list[HybridStoreCandidate]`
  - `term_hits`: `dict[str, int]` (used by the off-menu guard to detect zero-hit brands such as `"Folgers"`)

     - In `hybrid-search-turn`, compute `route_lex`, `route_vec`, `route_rrf`, `prod_lex`, `prod_vec`, `prod_rrf`, `store_lex`, and `store_rrf` CTEs with `1.0 / (:rrf_k + rank)` ($k = 60$) and `UNION ALL` them into a single result set ordered by `entity_type` and `rrf_score DESC`.
