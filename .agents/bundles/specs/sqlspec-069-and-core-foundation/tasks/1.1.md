## Objective

Upgrade `pyproject.toml` to `sqlspec[litestar,mypyc,opentelemetry,oracledb,performance]>=0.69.0` and `msgspec>=0.22.0`, align `DatabaseSettings` (`src/app/lib/settings/_database.py`) with SQLSpec `0.69.0` (`enable_session_tracing`, wire-level `sqlcommenter`, `queue_db` `"manage_lifespan": True`, and native `wallet_zip` without `os.environ["TNS_ADMIN"]` mutation or `check_hostname = False`), add `slots=True` to every settings dataclass, and remove dead `ADK_*` fields and duplicate lowercase `QueueSettings` properties.

## Context

- `pyproject.toml:21` currently specifies `"sqlspec[litestar,mypyc,oracledb,performance]>=0.65.0"` without an explicit `msgspec` floor, and `.gitignore:149-150` uses trailing slashes on `node_modules/` that miss symlinked worktree `node_modules`.
- `src/app/lib/settings/_database.py:125` sets `driver_features={"enable_AutoLob": True}`, which raises `ImproperConfigurationError` against SQLSpec `0.69.0`'s `VALID_ORACLE_DRIVER_FEATURE_KEYS`.
- `src/app/lib/settings/_database.py:149-168` (`get_queue_config`) omits `"manage_lifespan": True` in `extension_config["litestar"]`.
- `src/app/lib/settings/_database.py:174-200` (`_build_pool_config`) builds a manual `ssl.create_default_context()` with `ssl_ctx.check_hostname = False` and mutates `os.environ["TNS_ADMIN"]` instead of passing `"wallet_zip"` to SQLSpec `0.69.0`.
- `src/app/lib/settings/_database.py:52-60` and `tools/scripts/setup-env.sh:19-20` retain dead `ADK_IN_MEMORY`, `ADK_ENABLE_MEMORY`, and `ORACLE_ADK_IN_MEMORY` configuration/comments.
- `src/app/lib/settings/_queue.py:117-158` defines 8 lowercase `@property` wrappers (`enabled`, `backend`, `execution_backend`, `wakeup_mode`, `concurrency`, `poll_interval`, `manage_schema`, `in_memory`) duplicating `ENABLED`, `BACKEND`, `EXECUTION_BACKEND`, `WAKEUP_MODE`, `CONCURRENCY`, `POLL_INTERVAL`, `MANAGE_SCHEMA`, and `IN_MEMORY`.

## Notes & Discoveries

- Bare `OracleAsyncConfig` in `sqlspec==0.69.0` defaults `driver_features["fetch_lobs"]` to `False`; explicitly setting `driver_features={"fetch_lobs": True, "enable_session_tracing": True}` on `DatabaseSettings.create_config()` preserves automatic CLOB materialization while keeping `fetch_lobs: False` isolated on `DatabaseSettings.get_queue_config()`.
