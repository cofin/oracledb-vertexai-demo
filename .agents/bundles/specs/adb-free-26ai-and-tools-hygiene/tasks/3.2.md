## Objective

Retire the 7 legacy `gvenzl` SYSDBA APEX installer, ORDS sidecar, health, and SQLcl wrapper modules (`tools/oracle/{apex_media,apex_install,ords,health}.py`, `tools/oracle/cli/{ords,health,sqlcl}.py`), clean `tools/oracle/__init__.py`, `tools/oracle/cli/__init__.py`, and `manage.py`, add mTLS wallet (`TNS_ADMIN` + `myatp_low`) support to `ApexLang`, default `ApexCatalog` OpenAPI exports to `http://host.docker.internal:8000` with centralized serialization, and update `manage.py infra {start,status,wipe}` and `manage.py infra apex status` for `adb-free:latest-26ai`.

## Context

- `adb-free:latest-26ai` pre-installs APEX 26.1.5 and ORDS 26.3 on HTTPS `:8443` and disables `SYS AS SYSDBA`, making `apex_media.py`, `apex_install.py`, `ords.py`, `health.py`, and their CLI wrappers obsolete.
- `tools/oracle/apex_lang.py:28-192` omits `slots=True` on `ApexLangConfig` / `ApexLangResult` and always connects via `//{host}:{port}/freepdb1` without passing `TNS_ADMIN` to SQLcl when `WALLET_LOCATION` / `DATABASE_URL=myatp_low` is active.
- `tools/oracle/apex_catalog.py:8,56,68,80-94` calls `json.dumps` directly, performs a nested `from app.server.asgi import create_app`, and defaults unspecified `LITESTAR_HOST` (`0.0.0.0` / `localhost`) to `http://localhost:8000` (which points at the `adb-free` container itself when imported into APEX inside Docker, rather than `http://host.docker.internal:8000`).
- `manage.py:1-95` registers `oracle_ords_group` at line 83 and contains function-scoped imports.
