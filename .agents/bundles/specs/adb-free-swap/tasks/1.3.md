## Objective

On ADB-Free, `manage.py infra start` brings the database up without the gvenzl APEX installer or the ORDS sidecar and prints the bundled APEX URL, Database Actions URL, wallet directory, and service alias. `infra stop`, `remove`, and `status` never touch the ORDS sidecar. `infra apex install|upgrade|status` and every `infra ords` command refuse with a message that points to the bundled APEX and to `ORACLE_CONTAINER_FLAVOR=free`. On the free flavor every command behaves as today. `make start-infra` reuses an existing container.

## Context

- `tools/oracle/cli/database.py::_database_start()` calls `_auto_install_apex()` unless `--skip-apex` and `_start_ords()` unless `--skip-ords`; `_database_stop()` and `_database_remove()` always call `_build_ords_sidecar(db)`; `_database_status()` always prints the ORDS block. `_load_env_file(env_file)` loads `.env` with `override=True`.
- `tools/oracle/cli/apex.py`: `install`, `upgrade`, and `status` build the SYSDBA installer through `_build_installer()`; `export-openapi`, `generate`, `export`, `validate`, and `import` are SQLcl/APEXlang commands that stay untouched (D13).
- `tools/oracle/cli/ords.py`: `ords_group` with `start`, `stop`, `restart`, `status`, `logs`, `remove`, all gvenzl ORDS sidecar operations.
- `manage.py` maps `infra start|stop|restart|status|logs|wipe` to `tools.oracle.cli.database.database_group` and mounts `infra apex` and `infra ords`.
- `Makefile`: `start-infra` runs `manage.py infra start --recreate --skip-apex --skip-ords`; `apex` runs `manage.py infra start`.
- Task 1.1 added `ContainerFlavor` and the ADB `get_connection_info()` keys `apex_url`, `database_actions_url`, `wallet_location`, and `dsn`.
