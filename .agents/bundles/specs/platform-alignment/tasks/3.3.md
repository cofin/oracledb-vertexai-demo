## Objective

Adopt first-party `litestar-mcp>=0.15.0` agent, skill, prompt, and telemetry primitives and align `src/app/domain/ai/` with reference platform conventions by:
1. Creating `src/app/domain/ai/protocols/{__init__,_runtime}.py` defining runtime protocols (`SessionStoreProtocol`, `EventStoreProtocol`, `ModelClientProtocol`, `CoffeeToolContext`).
2. Replacing ad-hoc `CallContext.services: dict[str, Any]` lookups with a typed `CoffeeToolContext` dataclass and `RunContextRegistry` in `src/app/domain/ai/runtime/_context.py`.
3. Adopting `TurnBudget`, `BudgetExceededError`, `AgentStreamFrame`, and `SpanManager` from `litestar_mcp` in `src/app/domain/ai/runtime/{_loop,_stream}.py` while preserving our frontend-compatible `final` SSE payload enrichment.
4. Replacing `src/app/domain/ai/mcp/resources.py` with `CoffeeSkillController(SkillController)` in `src/app/domain/ai/mcp/_skills.py` and `CoffeePromptController(PromptController)` in `src/app/domain/ai/mcp/_prompts.py`.

## Context

- `litestar-mcp>=0.15.0` ships declarative `SkillController`, `PromptController`, `TurnBudget`, `BudgetExceededError`, `AgentStreamFrame`, and `SpanManager`.
- `src/app/domain/ai/mcp/resources.py` currently uses standalone functions that are not discovered by `AutowirePlugin` unless wrapped in `SkillController` / `PromptController` subclasses.
